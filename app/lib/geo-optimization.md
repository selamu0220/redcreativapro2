# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Strategy Guide

Based on deep research from Profound and SurferSEO (2025 Guides), here is the blueprint for optimizing content for LLM citations.

## Core Philosophy: "Citeability"
LLMs don't "rank" pages; they "synthesize" answers. To be cited, your content must be the most **authoritative** and **easy-to-parse** source of truth for a specific fact.

## The 9 GEO Pillars

### 1. Direct Answer Structuring (The "Snippet" Strategy)
LLMs prefer concise, definition-style answers.
- **Tactic:** Start sections with direct definition. `[Topic] is...`
- **Format:** `Query` -> `Direct Answer (40-60 words)` -> `Context/Nuance`.

### 2. Quotability & Unique Data (Data Density)
LLMs hallucinate less when provided with hard numbers.
- **Tactic:** Include unique statistics, percentages, or "coin" new terms (like "StealthWrite™").
- **Example:** Instead of "many people use AI," say "73% of enterprise marketers use AI..."

### 3. Authority Signals & Entity Association
LLMs rely on Knowledge Graphs.
- **Tactic:** Associate your brand with known entities (Wikidata, Crunchbase).
- **Tactic:** Use "Expert Quotes" deeply embedded in text.

### 4. Semantic Richness (NLP optimization)
Use semantically related entities, not just keywords.
- **Tool:** Use vector analysis to find "semantically close" topics.

### 5. Technical Structure for Machines
- **Schema:** Use `Article`, `FAQPage`, and `Speakable` schema.
- **Table of Contents:** Critical for LLM parsing structure.

### 6. "Fluency" Optimization
LLMs prioritize text that reads like "high-quality training data."
- **Paradox:** While we want to avoid AI detection for humans, for *optimization* we want high-clarity, structured text (ironically, good "AI-friendly" input).

### 7. Brand Citations > Backlinks
Mentions in authoritative text (news, whitepapers) matter more than dofollow links.
- **Strategy:** Digital PR to get "mentioned" as the source of a statistic.

## Proposed Tools for Red Creativa

### 1. The "GEO Analyzer" Tool
A tool that scans a text and scores it on "Citeability."
- **Check:** Does it have a direct answer definition?
- **Check:** Data Density Score (stats per 100 words).
- **Check:** Entity Density (named entities count).

### 2. "Fact Injection" Helper
A feature that suggests where to insert hard stats or quotes to increase "weight."

### 3. Schema Generator 2.0
Auto-generate specific `FAQPage` schema designed for LLM parsing.
